# QuickRestartBundle — Quickly restart your last task

I built a small Kimai plugin called **QuickRestartBundle** for people who frequently stop and restart tasks.

It adds a simple landing page with a **large Restart button in the middle**, allowing you to restart your most recent task with a single click.

### Features

* 🔄 Quickly restart your last task
* 🎯 Large, easy-to-use button
* 🏠 Can be configured as the **landing page after login**
* ⚡ Designed for workflows where you regularly switch between working and paused/stopped tasks
* 🧩 Small and focused — it doesn't change the normal Kimai workflow

The idea is to make the common **"I stopped working, now I want to continue"** action as quick as possible.
